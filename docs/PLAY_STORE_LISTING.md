Induction Program Past Papers is an educational preparation app for
teachers, teacher aspirants, and students preparing for the Khyber
Pakhtunkhwa Teacher Induction Program examinations.

**[IF REAL CONTENT EXISTS AND IS PUBLISHED FOR A GIVEN PHASE, use:]**
Browse original Phase II, Phase III, and Phase IV past papers exactly as
issued, with independently verified answer keys and fully solved
questions.

**[IF NO REAL CONTENT EXISTS YET FOR ANY PHASE (current status — see
`docs/REMAINING_WORK_AUDIT.md`), use this instead:]**
The app's phase/subject browsing structure is ready for Phase II, Phase
III, and Phase IV past papers as verified source material becomes
available — check back as content is added.

**What you can do:**

- **Original Papers** — view the exact scanned/source paper for each
  subject, exactly as issued.
- **Verified Answer Keys** — MCQ answer keys that have been
  independently checked against the correct answer, not just copied
  from the paper's own printed key.
- **Solved Questions** — fully worked short and long question answers.
- **Practice Mode** — timed MCQ practice sessions drawn from verified
  content, with instant scoring.
- **Search** — find questions by keyword across every published paper.
- **Bookmarks** — save questions and papers to revisit later.
- **Downloads** — save answer keys, solved questions, and original
  papers to your device for offline access, with a Downloads screen to
  manage them (open, share, delete).
- **AI Teacher** — an AI-powered study assistant that explains concepts
  in simple language, in English or Urdu, gives examples, generates
  practice quizzes, and offers exam tips. AI Teacher clearly labels its
  own responses as AI-generated and never presents them as official
  verified answers — when a question already has a verified answer in
  the app, AI Teacher explains that verified answer rather than
  replacing it.

**Who it's for:** newly recruited and aspiring KP government teachers,
and students studying teacher-certification material.

**Independence disclaimer:** This app is an independent educational
preparation resource. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, it is not an
official government application and is not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or operated by any government department.
