Privacy Policy

Induction Program Past Papers · Last reviewed against the app's actual code and database schema on 2026-09-13

Who this app is

Induction Program Past Papers is an independent educational preparation app for teachers, teacher aspirants, and students preparing for the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Teacher Induction Program exams. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, this app is not an official government application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government department.

Authentication & Supabase

Account creation, sign-in, and password reset are handled by Supabase Authentication. Your account data, bookmarks, practice history, progress, and AI Teacher conversations are stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database with Row Level Security enabled on every table that holds user data — you can only read or write your own rows; this is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app's screens.

AI Teacher

AI Teacher's responses always distinguish AI-generated content from the app's own independently verified answers with an explicit label — an AI response is never presented as an official verified answer. AI Teacher never receives your name, email, mobile number, or district; only the message you type and, if you opened it from a specific question, that question's own already-public content. The server-side function that powers AI Teacher does not log the content of your prompts or responses — only error events, which never include your message content or any credential.

Logging & error monitoring

The app's backend (Supabase Edge Functions) logs operational error events for reliability purposes — e.g. that a request failed and why, at a technical level. These logs do not include your AI Teacher message content, password, or any credential. We do not use a separate third-party crash-reporting or analytics SDK in the app itself.

Downloaded files

Files you download (answer keys, solved questions, original papers) are saved only in your device's own local app storage, listed in the app's Downloads screen. We do not track, upload, or receive any information about what you download.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. There is no automatic expiry on bookmarks, practice history, progress, or AI Teacher conversations — they persist until you delete them individually where the app supports that, or until you delete your account entirely.

Account deletion

You may delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the app. This permanently and irreversibly removes:

• Your profile (name, email, mobile number, district)
• Your bookmarks, practice attempts/answers, and progress
• Your AI Teacher conversations and messages (removed automatically as part of account deletion via the database's own referential rules)
• Your authentication account itself

The app also clears locally cached content and any files you've downloaded from your device at the same time. There is currently no separate "request deletion by email" process — deletion happens immediately and directly from within the app, which is the account-deletion method Google Play requires apps to offer.

Your rights

You can access and edit your own profile information (name, mobile number, district) directly in the app under Profile → Edit Profile. You can delete your bookmarks, practice history (via account deletion), and your account at any time as described above. If you have a request this app's own screens don't cover, contact us using the information below.

Security

Data is protected by Row Level Security at the database layer (see "Authentication & Supabase" above), and no service-role key, database credential, or AI provider key is ever included in the app itself — those exist only in the backend's own server environment.

Children's / student use

This app is intended for teachers, teacher aspirants, and students preparing for a professional teacher-certification exam — it is not directed at young children, and account creation requires the same information from every user regardless of age. If you believe a minor's data was collected inappropriately, contact us below and we will address it.
